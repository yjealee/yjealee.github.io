I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Government at Harvard University. I study comparative political behavior and the political economy of development, with a regional focus on Southeast Asia and Indonesia. My work asks how internal migration reshapes identities, intergroup relations, and urban politics. I use survey and field experiments, original fieldwork, and geospatial data. Before coming to Harvard, I completed a B.A. in Economics and Political Science and an M.A. in Political Science at Seoul National University.
