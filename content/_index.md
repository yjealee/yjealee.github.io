Welcome! I am a second-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Government at Harvard University. I study the political economy of development, with a focus on how people draw and navigate the boundaries of belonging (by ethnicity, religion, and place of residence) and what those boundaries mean for who invests in, trusts, and holds accountable the communities and states around them. My current work examines internal migration and urbanization in Indonesia, asking how the movement of people reshapes identities, intergroup relations, and local politics, both in fast-growing cities and in the hometowns migrants leave behind. I draw on survey and field experiments, original fieldwork, and large-scale behavioral data. Before Harvard, I received my B.A. in Economics and Political Science and my M.A. in Political Science from Seoul National University.
