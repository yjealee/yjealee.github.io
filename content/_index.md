Welcome! I am a second-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Government at Harvard University. I study the political economy of development, with a focus on how people draw and navigate the boundaries of belonging (by ethnicity, religion, and place of residence) and how these boundaries shape political behavior, intergroup relations, and development. My current work examines internal migration and urbanization in Indonesia, asking how the movement of people reshapes group identities and local politics, both in fast-growing cities and in the hometowns migrants leave behind. I draw on survey and field experiments, original fieldwork, and large-scale behavioral data. Before coming to Harvard, I received my B.A. in Economics and Political Science and my M.A. in Political Science from Seoul National University in South Korea.
