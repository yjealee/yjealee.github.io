Welcome! I am a second-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Government at Harvard University. I study the political economy of development, with a focus on how people draw and navigate the boundaries of belonging (by ethnicity, religion, and place of residence) and how these boundaries shape political behavior, intergroup relations, and development. My regional focus is Indonesia and Southeast Asia, with an interest in the Global South more broadly. I believe good questions begin in the field, so I spend as much time listening to people as I do working with data. Before coming to Harvard, I received my B.A. in Economics and Political Science and my M.A. in Political Science from Seoul National University in South Korea.
